Scrum Basics
What is Scrum?
Scrum is a framework that helps teams work together to solve complex problems and deliver valuable products in small, manageable steps.
The Three Scrum Accountabilities
Product Owner: Maximises product value and prioritises the Product Backlog.
Scrum Master: Helps the team understand Scrum and supports its effective use.
Developers: Create the product by completing the work required during a Sprint. What is a Sprint?
A Sprint is a fixed-length period of one month or less in which the team works towards a Sprint Goal.
 Scrum Events
Sprint Planning: The team plans the work and establishes the Sprint Goal.
Daily Scrum: Developers inspect progress towards the Sprint Goal and adapt their plan.
Sprint Review: The team and stakeholders inspect the outcome and discuss what to do next.
Sprint Retrospective: The team identifies ways to improve its effectiveness.
The Sprint: The container for all other Scrum events. Scrum Artifacts
Product Backlog: An ordered list of work needed to improve the product.
Sprint Backlog: The Sprint Goal, selected Product Backlog items, and the plan for delivering them.
Increment: A usable step towards the product goal that meets the Definition of Done.
Practical Example: Website Design
Project: Design a website for a small business.
Sprint Goal: Create the basic website design.
Tasks:
Gather website requirements.
Create a sitemap.
Design the homepage wireframe in Figma.
Design the contact page.
Review the designs and collect feedback.
